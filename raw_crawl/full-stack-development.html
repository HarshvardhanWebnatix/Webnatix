AI solutions

Discursia

Discursia, an AI-powered content curation platform, sought to improve trend analysis, content filtering, and user engagement. We developed an AI-driven scraping system, machine learning algorithms, and a profanity filtering module to enhance content recommendations and ensure data accuracy.

  • Challenges

Managing large real-time data volumes, distinguishing between fleeting and impactful trends, and moderating content were key hurdles. Ensuring high-quality insights while filtering inappropriate material was essential to maintaining credibility.

  • Solution

We implemented an AI-based data extraction system, machine learning algorithms for trend analysis, and a profanity filtering module for cleaner content. These enhancements improved engagement, refined content recommendations, and positioned Discursia as a trusted leader.

  • TECHNOLOGY Python, Gemini, Flask, Sentiments analysis

AI solutions

VIPR

Vipr is an AI-driven safety platform that enhances workplace safety in high-risk industries like mining, education, and disability support. By integrating real-time sensor data with machine learning, it automates hazard detection, predicts risks, and provides tailored safety recommendations.

  • Challenges

Traditional safety systems relied on manual monitoring, leading to delayed hazard detection and increased risks. These methods lacked predictive capabilities and struggled to meet industry-specific safety needs. Adapting to evolving regulatory standards was also a challenge for many organizations.

  • Solution

We developed an AI-powered platform that automates hazard detection using computer vision and predictive analytics. It delivers tailored safety recommendations and integrates seamlessly with existing safety infrastructures, enabling proactive risk management and regulatory compliance.

  • TECHNOLOGY OpenAI, FullStack, ReactNative

AI solutions

Man with Van Slough

Man with Van Slough, a trusted moving service provider in the UK, sought to improve website accessibility and engagement. The text-heavy website made it difficult for users with visual impairments or those preferring auditory content. To enhance accessibility, we integrated Google Text-to-Speech (GTTS), allowing visitors to listen to website content, improving user experience and engagement

  • Challenges

The website lacked accessibility features, limiting usability for visually impaired users and those who preferred auditory content. Low user engagement and a text-heavy interface reduced visitor interaction. Implementing new features without disrupting the existing website structure was another concern

  • Solution

We integrated Google GTTS for real-time text-to-speech conversion, enabling seamless audio accessibility without modifying the backend. The feature was embedded efficiently, ensuring smooth performance while maintaining the website’s structure. This enhancement improved usability, increased engagement, and positioned the company as an inclusive service provider.

  • TECHNOLOGY Google Text-to-Speech (GTTS), Python, Cloud-Based Integration.

AI solutions

MTC GS Limited

MTC GS Limited required a secure and efficient employee verification system to prevent fraud, enhance data accuracy, and meet security standards. Manual verification methods were prone to errors, leading to security risks and inefficiencies. To address these challenges, we developed a biometric authentication system using fingerprint and facial recognition, streamlining onboarding and ensuring compliance with strict security regulations

  • Challenges

Traditional verification methods led to inaccuracies, making it easier for unauthorized individuals to access sensitive data. Managing duplicate records was inefficient, and ensuring compliance with evolving security standards was difficult with outdated manual processes.

  • Solution

We integrated fingerprint and facial recognition technology for precise employee verification. The system automates background checks by linking with criminal record databases, eliminating duplicate entries, and preventing fraudulent access. This advanced biometric solution ensures secure employee onboarding while maintaining data integrity

  • TECHNOLOGY FaceNet library, Docker, MTCNN, SecuGen library, Flask, ChromaDB, Full-Stack

AI solutions

FlextIn

FlextIn is a forward-thinking organization focused on modernizing workplace operations with advanced technology. The company aimed to replace outdated attendance systems with a secure, contactless, and automated solution. We developed an AI-powered facial recognition system to enhance security, streamline attendance tracking, and improve workplace efficiency.

  • Challenges

Manual attendance systems caused delays, inaccuracies, and security risks due to lost or stolen access cards. HR teams faced an increased workload correcting errors, while peak-hour congestion disrupted productivity. A faster, more secure, and automated solution was needed.

  • Solution

We implemented an AI-driven facial recognition system for seamless, contactless authentication. It improved security, eliminated manual errors, and reduced administrative workload, ensuring efficient workplace access.

  • TECHNOLOGY Python, FaceNet Library, MTCNN, Flask, ChromaDB

AI solutions

Mintz Group

Mintz Group is a leading provider of employee background checks and criminal record verification services. The company sought to enhance its verification workflows by automating data extraction, structuring, and processing. We developed an intelligent system that streamlined data handling, improved accuracy, and enabled faster background verification.

  • Challenges

Manual data extraction from multiple sources was time-consuming, causing delays in verification. The unstructured data required extensive reformatting, increasing the risk of errors. As data volumes grew, scalability became a concern, and error-prone workflows affected the accuracy of reports.

  • Solution

We implemented an automated system that extracts, structures, and formats data seamlessly. Intelligent parsing algorithms reduced errors, while a ZIP file download feature optimized data storage and retrieval, improving efficiency

  • TECHNOLOGY Python, Manticore Search, Flask, FFmpeg, Celery, Selenium, Wayback Archive

HIRE AS PER YOUR REQUIREMENT

Get Our Assistance for Your Business Needs

At Dotsquares, we provide flexible options for accessing our developers' time, allowing you to choose the duration and frequency of their availability based on your specific requirements.

Bucket hours

When you buy bucket hours, you purchase a set number of hours upfront.

  • Your purchased bucket hours remain valid for 6 months, during this time frame, you can utilize our services until your hours are exhausted or until the 6-month period expires.
  • For example, if you invest in 40 bucket hours and use 10 hours within the first month, you will have a remaining 30 hours to utilize over the next 5 months.
  • In this case, the developer will work for other projects simultaneously as you have opted for bucket hours and not dedicated hiring.

It's a convenient and efficient way to manage your developer needs on your schedule.

Explore more
Dedicated/Regular Hiring

In dedicated hiring, the number of hours are not fixed like the bucket hours but instead, you are reserving the developer exclusively for your project.

  • The developer will work only on your project for a set amount of time.
  • You can choose to hire the developer for a week or a month, depending on what your project needs.
  • This means our developer will focus exclusively on meeting the needs of your project, without any distractions from other commitments.

Whether you need help for a short time or a longer period, our dedicated hiring option ensures your project gets the attention it deserves.
