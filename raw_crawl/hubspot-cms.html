CRM/CMS Development

HubSpot-QuickBooks Integration: Automating Invoicing and Payment Management

  • Challenges

Bonham & Brook faced limitations with HubSpot's native integration with QuickBooks, including no support for custom objects or proforma invoices, reliance on manual processes for invoicing and payment tracking, high Stripe transaction fees, and challenges in maintaining accurate data synchronization.

  • Solution

Developed a custom middleware application to automate invoicing, payment management, and data synchronization between HubSpot and QuickBooks. - Automated proforma invoice generation and email delivery in HubSpot. - Streamlined actual invoice creation in QuickBooks triggered by service completion.

  • Business Impact

Reduced manual effort in invoice creation and payment tracking. Improved financial data accuracy and synchronization between systems. Lowered transaction costs by using QuickBooks over Stripe.

  • TECHNOLOGY HubSpot
  • Region USA
